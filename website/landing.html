Import an image, PDF or DWG with Claude. Watch accepted walls and rooms appear in your live project, compare the detected vectors with the original drawing, and guide the work through chat.

Set the scale

Supply a known width, depth or footprint area. Add the building type, floor count and instructions for the drawing.

A MODEL YOU CAN BUILD ON

Keep spaces connected.
Keep your application yours.

Walls share junctions. Spaces reference their boundaries. Doors belong to walls, and portals describe the connections between spaces. The viewer builds its 3D surfaces from that model.

Read the geometry guide ↗
01

Edit with confidence

Lightweight drag previews stay responsive. Each drop is validated as a transaction; an invalid edit restores the previous plan. Undo, redo and JSON export keep work recoverable.

02

Bring your live data

Bind objects to host-owned feed IDs. Show device states and operate elevators through adapters while keeping your business rules in your own application.

03

Import and extend

Bring in CAD geometry, geospatial footprints and reference drawings. Customize the theme, icons and panels, or use the schema without a browser.
